Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Agent Readiness HQ ("we," "us," or "our") provides website assessment and implementation services. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we handle it. We collect only what we need to deliver our services.

1. Information we collect

  • Free audit: the website URL you enter. No account and no email required.
  • Email capture (optional): if you choose to share your email address to receive a sample report or service details, we store that email address to send what you requested.
  • Purchases: your name, business name, website URL, and email address, plus order details. Payment card details are processed directly by our payment provider (Stripe). We never see or store your full card number.
  • $149 / $449 intake: business facts you provide so we can build your AI Information Page (services, hours, pricing, service areas, contact details, credentials). We use only what you verify.
  • Public business information: information already published on your website, which our audit reads in order to score it.
  • Campaign attribution: when you arrive via our direct-mail QR code, we record that the visit came from the mail campaign (campaign-level only; we do not use personalized QR codes or per-recipient tracking).
  • Contact messages: your name, email, and message content when you contact us.

2. How we use it

  • To run the audit you requested and show you the results.
  • To deliver purchased services: reports, AI Information Pages, implementation, and monitoring.
  • To communicate with you about your order or inquiry (confirmations, intake requests, completion notices).
  • To measure, in aggregate, whether our services and campaigns are working (for example, how many mail recipients ran a scan).
  • To process audits with AI assistance, which helps us analyze website content and draft findings. Every report is reviewed before it is delivered.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information to anyone, and we do not share it for targeted advertising.
  • Providing your email for an audit, inquiry, or purchase does not automatically enroll you in ongoing marketing. We only send marketing if you separately opt in.
  • This is a simple static website. We do not run advertising cookies, cross-site trackers, or third-party analytics profiles on you.
  • We do not ask for, and you should never send us, passwords or login credentials by email, chat, or form.

4. Service providers

We use a small number of service providers to operate: our website host and our payment processor (Stripe, which handles card data under its own privacy policy). We share only the information each provider needs to perform its function.

5. Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including limiting access to the people who need it to do their jobs and using encrypted connections. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but we keep our practices proportionate to the limited data we hold.

6. Data retention

We keep information only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected. In practice, that means: audit records and order information are kept while we provide the service and for a reasonable period afterward to resolve disputes, handle refunds, and meet tax and accounting obligations. Monitoring subscription records are kept for the life of the subscription plus the period needed for billing disputes. If you ask us to delete your personal information, we will do so unless we are required to keep it for legal or accounting reasons, in which case we will tell you what we are keeping and why.

7. Your choices

You can run the free scan without providing any personal information. You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it by contacting us through the contact information on our website. If you opted in to marketing emails, every message includes a way to opt out.

8. Children's information

Our services are directed at businesses, not children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us through the contact information on our website and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time as our services grow. Material changes will be noted by updating the date above, and we will notify active customers by email when a change materially affects how we handle their information. Continued use of our services after changes take effect constitutes acceptance.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or your personal information: please use the contact information on our website.
