How AI agents find (or miss) your business online

October 10, 2026

A growing number of buying decisions now start with a question to an AI assistant, not a Google search. "Find me a plumber near me with good reviews." "What does a website redesign cost?" The assistant answers from what it can find and understand online. If your business is not machine-readable, you are not in the running.

What agents actually need

AI agents do not browse like people. They do not admire your hero image or read between the lines of clever copy. They need structured facts: your business name, services, prices, hours, location, and contact info, in formats they can parse. That means schema markup, an llms.txt file, pricing in the page HTML, a sitemap, and a robots.txt that does not block them.

Why businesses get missed

  • Prices live in images. AI cannot read text baked into a graphic. If your pricing is a JPEG, it does not exist to an agent.
  • No structured data. Without schema.org markup, the agent has to guess what your business does from prose. Guesses favor whoever made it explicit.
  • JavaScript-only content. Prices or service lists that only render after JavaScript runs are invisible to simpler crawlers.
  • Blocked crawlers. Some sites block AI crawlers in robots.txt without realizing it, often because a security plugin did it by default.

The good news

Almost all of this is fixable in an afternoon. None of it requires a redesign. It is plumbing, not paint: small files and snippets that make your existing site legible to machines. That is exactly what our audit measures, across seven checks, in under a minute.
