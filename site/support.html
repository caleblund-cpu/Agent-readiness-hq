What is an AI agent?

An AI agent is software that acts on someone's behalf: researching options, comparing prices, filling carts, even completing purchases. Think of it as a tireless personal assistant that does the shopping for the human. When your customer sends an agent instead of visiting your site themselves, the agent's experience is your customer's experience.

Will this help my SEO?

Yes, it might. Most of what makes a website easy for AI agents to read is the same housekeeping search engines have rewarded for years: a clear site structure, accurate structured data, content that is easy to access, and a useful sitemap. Getting your facts straight for machines usually makes your site clearer for everyone, search engines included. What we will never do is promise specific rankings or traffic. Nobody honest can.

What if I block AI crawlers on purpose?

That can be a legitimate choice. AI crawlers and automated systems serve different purposes, and blocking one does not necessarily mean blocking all AI-related access. Our scan identifies relevant crawler-access settings so you can make an informed decision about which automated systems you choose to allow.
