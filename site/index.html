See what AI finds when it reads your site

The Free Scan is our proprietary tool. It shows what may keep AI agents from fully understanding your website, in seven plain-English questions:

  1. Are AI agents welcome in, or turned away at the door before they can look around?
  2. Does your business introduce itself in the clear, plain way AI agents read best?
  3. Can an agent find every page that matters, or does it only ever see a corner of your site?
  4. Are your services and details stated clearly enough for a machine to repeat them accurately?
  5. Can an agent find what things cost? If prices aren't stated in a form it can read, it may move on without them.
  6. Does anything on your site stop an agent cold before it learns a single thing about you?
  7. Do your pages load quickly enough for an agent working through a long list of businesses?

All seven checks are technical. The good news is we do the work: we can recommend the fixes, or do them for you.
