How the Agent Readiness HQ score works

For twenty years, customers found businesses by searching, clicking, and browsing. That was the attention economy: win the click, win the customer. Now AI agents are starting to do the searching and comparing for them. That is the agent economy, and it asks a different first question: can an AI agent understand your business well enough to recommend it?

One score, 0–100, with a letter grade, based on our proprietary tool built in-house for one job: finding the problems that keep AI agents from understanding a business. It runs seven checks on what an AI agent runs into when it comes looking:

  1. The welcome: whether AI agents are allowed in to look around, or turned away at the door.
  2. The introduction: whether your business introduces itself in the clear, plain way AI agents read best.
  3. The tour: whether an AI agent can find every page that matters, or only ever sees a corner of your site.
  4. The facts: whether your services and details are stated clearly enough for a machine to repeat them accurately.
  5. The prices: whether an AI agent can find what things cost. It will not guess. It will move on.
  6. The roadblocks: whether anything on your site stops an AI agent cold before it learns a single thing about you.
  7. The wait: whether your pages load quickly enough for an AI agent working through a long list of businesses.

Under the hood, all seven are technical: code, structure, and how your site is put together. That is the work we do, first by measuring it, then by fixing it.
