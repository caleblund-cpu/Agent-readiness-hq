Frequently Asked Questions

The free scan and your score

What is an AI agent?

An AI agent is software that acts on someone's behalf: researching options, comparing prices, and booking. More and more, it starts with a customer asking AI a question, and the AI naming the businesses it can read clearly.

What does the free scan check, in plain English?
  1. Can AI systems get into your site, or are they turned away at the door?
  2. Does your business introduce itself in the clear way AI reads best?
  3. Can AI find every page that matters, or only a corner of your site?
  4. Are your services and details clear enough for a machine to repeat accurately?
  5. Can AI find what things cost? Prices it cannot read may get skipped.
  6. Does anything stop AI cold before it learns anything about you?
  7. Do your pages load fast enough for a system working through a long list?

You get a 0 to 100 score plus a fix list for each check. All seven checks are technical, and we can recommend the fixes, or do them for you.

Your score runs from 0 to 100, with a letter grade (A 90 and above, B 80 to 89.9, C 70 to 79.9, D 60 to 69.9, F below 60). It reflects how easy your site is for AI to read, not how pretty it looks or how good your business is. It is a snapshot from the day of the scan; a later scan can differ if your site or our methodology has changed.

What is an AI Information Page?

One page on your website that brings your key facts together in one place: services, pricing, hours, service area, and contact details, written so AI can read and cite them accurately. Under both plans it is built from the facts you confirm.

What is the difference between the free scan, Basic, and Plus?

The free scan covers all seven checks with a plain-language fix list, right on our homepage. The paid plans start with the full recommended change document for your website, prioritized in plain English, and then keep checking how AI answers talk about your business, every month.

Who does the work. The change document and the monitoring are the same either way. With Basic, you make the changes using our monthly recommended edits. With Plus, we implement up to 3 approved improvements a month for you, each approved by you first, under one standing authorization.

How does the timing work?

The free scan itself runs in under a minute: you get your 0–100 score plus the fix list for each check right away. Under either plan, your change document comes first, in your first month. Monitoring then runs monthly. Under Plus, improvements are made monthly, after your approval, up to 3 per month.

Which AI systems do you check, and how often?

Every month we sample how ChatGPT, Google AI, Claude, and Perplexity answer common customer questions about your business, and a human reviews what we find before your Snapshot is written. We add new AI systems as access to them opens up.

It is a measurement of what we observed, not a ranking or guarantee of how any AI system will answer in the future.

One month's answers reflect your website as it stands then. After that, your business keeps changing: services, hours, and prices get updated, and the AI tools that read your site change how they work. Monthly checks catch that drift while it is still small, and the recommended edits, or the Plus improvements, keep your facts current. Monitoring does not promise any particular result in AI answers.

Platforms and limits

What if my website platform limits some of the fixes?

Our goal is to make your site as easy for AI to read as your platform allows. If a fix cannot be done on your platform or plan, we tell you plainly and focus on what will work. One honest consequence: if your platform cannot support a component the scan checks for, a hosted platform that won't allow a root-level llms.txt file, for example, the scan cannot award those points, so a score on that platform may cap below 100 (a platform that blocks llms.txt may cap at 90). We identify that ceiling in your free scan results, before you buy.

What if I block AI crawlers on purpose?

That can be a legitimate choice. AI crawlers and automated systems serve different purposes, and blocking one does not necessarily mean blocking all AI-related access. Our scan identifies relevant crawler-access settings so you can make an informed decision about which automated systems you choose to allow.
