What Agent Readiness HQ does

Many customers now use AI agents and AI search tools to find and compare local businesses. If a website is hard for those systems to read, the business may be skipped or described inaccurately. Agent Readiness HQ checks how machine-readable a business website is, explains the findings in plain English, and provides or implements the fixes. This is not a website redesign. We add the machine-readable layer underneath.

The free AI Readiness Scan

The free scan checks a website across seven technical factors and produces a score from 0 to 100 with a letter grade:

  1. robots.txt AI-crawler posture: whether the site's robots file allows known AI crawlers.
  2. llms.txt: whether the site offers a summary file written for AI systems.
  3. Sitemap: whether the site publishes a sitemap listing its pages.
  4. Schema.org JSON-LD: whether the site describes itself in structured data machines can parse, including its business type, products, and prices.
  5. Pricing in raw HTML: whether prices appear in the page's raw text, where automated readers can find them.
  6. Bot-wall: whether the site puts challenge walls in front of automated visitors at the door.
  7. Page speed: how quickly the site starts responding and finishes loading.

Scores: A is 90 to 100, B is 75 to 89, C is 60 to 74, F is below 60. The scan is free and runs instantly on the website. The score is an assessment of technical machine-readability only. It does not guarantee increased traffic, customer acquisition, revenue, AI rankings, or citations.
