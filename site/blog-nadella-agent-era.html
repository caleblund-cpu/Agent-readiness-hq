Microsoft's CEO says business apps will collapse in the agent era. Your website is an app, too.

September 15, 2026

In December 2024, Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella went on the BG2 podcast and said the quiet part about software out loud. The notion that business applications exist, he said, is "probably where they'll all collapse, in the agent era." Most business software, in his telling, is really just databases with business logic attached, and "the business logic is all going to these agents."

Strip away the jargon and his point is simple. Software was built for humans to click through. Agents do not click. They connect, read the underlying facts, and act. When the customer is an agent, an interface designed for human eyes stops being the product. The information underneath becomes the product.

Your website has the same design problem

A typical small business website is business software in miniature, built the same way: pages designed to look good to a person, with the real facts scattered, implied, or missing. What you do is in a hero image. Your service area is "and surrounding areas." Your pricing is "call for a quote." A human visitor can squint and figure it out. An agent cannot, and it will not try for long.

This is the shift the agent economy actually demands of a local business. Not a new website. A website whose facts an agent can extract and trust, the same way Nadella's agents will extract facts from the databases behind business software.

What to do about it

Start by seeing your site the way an agent does. Are your services, location, hours, and contact details stated in plain, consistent text? Is there a file that tells AI systems what your business is? Does anything on your site turn agents away at the door? Those are checkable things, and most of them are quick to fix once you know where you stand.
