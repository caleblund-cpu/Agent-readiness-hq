Your next customer may be an AI agent

An AI agent is software that works on behalf of a person. It searches, compares, and buys, the way a very fast, very literal assistant would. People already ask tools like ChatGPT and Gemini to find a plumber, compare prices, and book the job, and those tools send agents to read business websites and report back.

Agents cannot be persuaded by marketing, and they do not guess. They read what a machine can read: clear services, prices, locations, hours, and business facts, stated in ways software can parse. If your website hides those facts inside images, scripts, or pages an agent cannot reach, the agent moves on to a competitor it can understand. That is what machine readability means, and it is the whole problem we work on.

How an agent decides: ask, research, act

?

1. Ask

A customer asks an AI tool for help: find a roofer near me, compare two services, book the cheaper one.

◎

2. Research

The agent visits business websites and reads what it can: services, pricing, service area, reviews, and contact details.

✓

3. Act

The agent recommends, contacts, or books the businesses it could understand with confidence, and skips the rest.

Your website is your entry in that process. If an agent can read it clearly, you are in the running. If it cannot, you were never considered.

Step one: the free scan

Our free scan runs seven checks on your website, the same things an AI agent runs into when it comes looking: whether agents are allowed in, whether your business introduces itself clearly, whether every important page can be found, whether your facts are stated in machine-readable form, whether your prices are visible, whether anything blocks an agent at the door, and whether your pages load fast enough.

You get one score from 0 to 100, with a letter grade: A at 90 and up, B at 75 and up, C at 60 and up, and F below 60. The score is a measure of technical machine readability only. A high score means agents can read you. A low score shows exactly where they get stuck, which is what the paid plans fix.
