FAQs

The free scan and your score

What is an AI agent?

An AI agent is software that acts on someone's behalf: researching options, comparing prices, filling forms, and booking. When customers ask an AI agent to find a local business, the agent reads websites the way a very fast, very literal person would.

What does the free scan check?

The scan runs seven checks on your website, looking at the same things an AI agent looks for: whether your key facts are easy to find, whether your pages load quickly, whether your site works on phones, and more. You get a 0–100 score plus a fix list for each check.

How does the score work?

Your score runs from 0 to 100, with a letter grade (A at 90 and above, B at 75, C at 60, F below 60). The score reflects how easy your site is for AI agents to read and act on — not how pretty it looks.

It is one new page added to your existing website that brings your key business facts together in one place — services, pricing, hours, service area, and contact details — written so AI agents can read and cite them accurately.

No. Payment is not authorization. Paying buys the service; it never gives us permission to change your site. For Done For You, you sign a separate authorization and approve every change before we make it.

Platforms and limits

What if my website platform limits some of the fixes?

Our goal is to make your site as easy for AI agents to read as your platform allows. If a fix cannot be done on your platform or plan, we tell you plainly and focus on what will work.

What if I block AI crawlers on purpose?

That can be a legitimate choice. AI crawlers and automated systems serve different purposes, and blocking one does not necessarily mean blocking all AI-related access. Our scan identifies relevant crawler-access settings so you can make an informed decision about which automated systems you choose to allow.
