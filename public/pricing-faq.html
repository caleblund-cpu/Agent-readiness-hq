Frequently Asked Questions

The free scan and your score

What is an AI agent?

An AI agent is software that acts on someone's behalf: researching options, comparing prices, filling carts, even completing purchases. Think of it as a tireless personal assistant that does the shopping for the human. When your customer sends an agent instead of visiting your site themselves, the agent's experience is your customer's experience.

What does the free scan check?

The scan runs seven checks on your website, looking at the same things an AI agent looks for: whether agents are allowed in, whether your business introduces itself clearly, whether every important page can be found, whether your services and details are stated in a machine-readable way, whether your prices can be found, whether anything blocks an agent at the door, and whether your pages load fast enough. You get a score from 0 to 100 with a letter grade, your results on screen and by email, and the top areas to review. It is free, and we never ask for payment details to run it.

How does the score work?

Your score runs from 0 to 100, with a letter grade (A at 90 and above, B at 75, C at 60, F below 60). Each of the seven checks carries a different weight, based on how much it may affect an agent's ability to read your business accurately. The score measures technical machine-readability only. It is not a ranking, not a review of your business, and not a prediction of traffic. On some website platforms, certain checks cannot be implemented at all, so your results may also show a maximum possible score for your platform, next to your current score.

Platforms and limits

What if my website platform limits some of the fixes?

Our goal is to make your site as easy for AI agents to read as your platform allows. If a fix can be done on your platform, we implement it or give you the exact steps and code to do it. The only limits are the platform's own: some hosted platforms restrict how much code anyone can add, and no one can implement what the platform itself blocks. Implementation depends on your website platform and plan. If you run the free scan first, we'll identify any platform limitations before you purchase, so you'll know what we can and can't implement. Some platforms, such as hosted website builders, do not allow certain items at all. Your free scan flags those before you buy anything, and your report explains what your platform prevents and what the closest alternative is. This is also why your results may show a maximum possible score for your platform: when a platform does not allow certain items to be added at all, no matter who does the work, your score has a ceiling. That ceiling is a limit of the platform, not a failure of your site. If a platform blocks so much that Done For You would not deliver real value, we will not sell it to you; we will offer the DIY Audit instead and explain the limits in that report. We work with most common platforms, including WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, Shopify, Webflow, Kajabi, Duda, and GoDaddy.
