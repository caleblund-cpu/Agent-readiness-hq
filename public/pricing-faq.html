Frequently Asked Questions

The free scan and your score

What is an AI agent?

An AI agent is software that acts on someone's behalf: researching options, comparing prices, filling forms, and booking. When customers ask an AI agent to find a local business, the agent reads websites the way a very fast, very literal person would.

What does the free scan check?

The scan runs seven checks on your website, looking at the same things an AI agent looks for: whether your key facts are easy to find, whether your pages load quickly, whether your site works on phones, and more. You get a 0–100 score plus a fix list for each check.

How does the score work?

Your score runs from 0 to 100, with a letter grade (A at 90 and above, B at 75, C at 60, F below 60). The score reflects how easy your site is for AI agents to read and act on — not how pretty it looks.
