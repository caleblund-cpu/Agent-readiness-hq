Frequently Asked Questions

The free scan and your score

What is an AI agent?

An AI agent is software that acts on someone's behalf: researching options, comparing prices, filling forms, and booking. When customers ask an AI agent to find a local business, the agent reads websites the way a very fast, very literal person would.

What does the free scan check?

The scan runs seven technical checks on your website: whether AI crawlers are allowed in, whether your business has an llms.txt introduction file, whether your pages can all be found, whether your facts are stated in structured data machines can read, whether your prices are visible, whether anything blocks automated access at the door, and whether your pages respond quickly. You get a 0 to 100 score plus a fix list for each check.

How does the score work?

Your score runs from 0 to 100, with a letter grade (A at 90 and above, B from 80 to below 90, C from 70 to below 80, D from 60 to below 70, F below 60). The score reflects how easy your site is for AI agents to read and act on, not how pretty it looks, and not the quality of your business. It is a snapshot from the day of the scan, under the methodology version then in use; a later scan can differ if your site, scan conditions, or our methodology has changed.

It is one new page added to your existing website that brings your key business facts together in one place — services, pricing, hours, service area, and contact details — written so AI agents can read and cite them accurately.

Platforms and limits

What if my website platform limits some of the fixes?

Our goal is to make your site as easy for AI agents to read as your platform allows. If a fix cannot be done on your platform or plan, we tell you plainly and focus on what will work. One honest consequence: if your platform cannot support a component the scan checks for, a hosted platform that won't allow a root-level llms.txt file, for example, the scan cannot award those points, so a score on that platform may cap below 100 (a platform that blocks llms.txt may cap at 90). We identify that ceiling in your free scan results, before you buy.

What if I block AI crawlers on purpose?

That can be a legitimate choice. AI crawlers and automated systems serve different purposes, and blocking one does not necessarily mean blocking all AI-related access. Our scan identifies relevant crawler-access settings so you can make an informed decision about which automated systems you choose to allow.
