AI does not sit still

The assistants your customers ask are updated constantly. Platforms roll out new features, change how pages are exposed to machines, and retire old ones. Your own business changes too: new services, new hours, new prices.

A cleanup reflects your site on the day it shipped. Over time, small changes could leave your AI Information Page out of date, and details that were accurate and machine-readable may stop matching the business you run today. None of this announces itself. Without a regular check, drift is easy to miss until it has been sitting there for months.

Why we report trends, not scores

Ask the same question twice and you may get two different answers. AI assistants assemble each answer on the spot, and the businesses they name can change from one day to the next. In one 2026 study, the same question asked twice produced the identical list of businesses less than 1 percent of the time. A single check on a single afternoon could show almost anything, so we do not grade your business on one answer, and we never sell a ranking.

Instead, each monthly check asks a wide set of real customer questions and follows the direction over time: how often your business shows up, and whether the facts in the answers are right. Wrong hours, an old price, or a service you no longer offer get flagged for correction. A trend across months tells you something true. One score, from one day, possibly tells you nothing at all.
