Our services

If it heats or cools your home, we work on it. Every repair starts with a diagnostic visit and a flat quote, and we only go ahead when you approve the price.

Furnace repair

No heat, strange noises, or a furnace that keeps shutting off. We diagnose the problem, show you the flat repair price, and fix most furnaces on the first visit.

Air conditioning repair

Warm air, weak airflow, or a unit that will not start. We service all major brands and carry the common parts on the truck.

New system installation

When a system is at the end of its life, we size a replacement for your home and give you a free in home estimate with the full price in writing, plus financing options.

Seasonal tune ups

A 21 point check of your furnace or air conditioner for $89 per system, including a safety and carbon monoxide check and a written report on the health of your system.

24/7 emergency service

No heat on a cold night is not something to sleep on. Our emergency line is answered around the clock, every day of the year.

Heat pumps and indoor air

Heat pump and ductless mini split service and installation, plus humidifiers, air purifiers, and smart thermostats for dry Colorado air.

About High Meadow

High Meadow Heating & Air has been family owned and operated since 2012. We are licensed and insured in Colorado, our technicians are background checked, and most of our work comes from repeat customers and referrals in Douglas County. We service all major brands, including Trane, Carrier, Lennox, Rheem, Goodman, Bryant, American Standard, York, and Amana, and we install Trane and Rheem systems. Every repair is backed by a 2 year parts and labor warranty. New systems include a 10 year manufacturer parts warranty and a 2 year labor warranty from us.
